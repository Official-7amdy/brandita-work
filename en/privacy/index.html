The short version

  • We only collect what you type into our forms — the consultation form and the newsletter sign-up — plus the technical details any website receives when you visit it.
  • Form submissions are emailed to our team. The website doesn't keep its own database of them.
  • A few service providers handle this for us: Cloudflare, which hosts and protects the site, Resend, which delivers our email, and the company that hosts our team inbox.
  • The site's own code sets no cookies, and we run no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell your information. You can ask to see, correct or delete it at any time.

02 What we collect

When you ask for a consultation

Our consultation form asks for:

  • your full name, work email, and phone or WhatsApp number
  • your company name, and your website or store address if you choose to share it
  • your country or main market
  • your monthly marketing budget, and when you’d like to start
  • what you need help with, what success would look like for you in six months, and anything else you choose to tell us

The fuller form on our contact page also asks for your job title, your industry, your approximate monthly revenue (you can choose not to say), whether you’re running paid ads today and, optionally, how you heard about us.

With your answers, we also receive the page you sent the form from and the time you sent it.

If you choose a monthly budget under $900, the form shrinks to a single email field. In that case we receive only your email address, the budget you chose and the page you were on, so we can send you the material you asked for.

When you subscribe to our newsletter

We collect your email address and the time you signed up. Nothing else.

When you contact us directly

If you email us or message us on WhatsApp, we receive what you send us, along with your email address or phone number.

When you visit the site

Like every website, ours receives some technical information each time your browser asks it for a page: your IP address, your browser’s user agent (which tells us the type of browser and device), the address of the page requested and the time of the request.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, uses this to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. When you submit a form, the request is handled by our own code running on Cloudflare, and Cloudflare’s logging records details of that request — such as when it happened, whether it worked and any error messages — so we can find and fix problems.

We don’t use this information to identify you or to build a profile of you.

What we don’t ask for

We don’t need sensitive information, such as details about your health, religion or political views. Please don’t include any in the free-text fields.

03 How we use it, and why we’re allowed to

Data protection law expects us to have a valid reason — a “legal basis” — for each way we use personal information. Here is what we use your information for, and our reason for each.

Consultation form

  • What we use it for: Replying to you, preparing for and holding the consultation call, and, if we’re a fit, writing you a proposal.
  • Our reason: You asked us to: these are steps you’ve requested before a possible agreement. It’s also in our legitimate interest to respond to business enquiries.

Email left with a budget under $900

  • What we use it for: Sending you the material you asked for, and getting back in touch when the timing is right.
  • Our reason: Your request, and your consent to hear from us again. You can withdraw it at any time.

Newsletter email

  • What we use it for: Sending you our newsletter.
  • Our reason: Your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.

Technical and log data

  • What we use it for: Delivering the site, keeping it secure, filtering out spam and fixing errors.
  • Our reason: Our legitimate interest in running a secure website that works.

We may also keep or use information where the law requires us to, or where we need it to establish or defend a legal claim.

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it with advertisers.

04 Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run this website. They handle information on our behalf, only to provide their service to us.

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, delivers it through its global network, protects it from attacks and abuse, runs the code that handles our forms, and keeps the request logs described above. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
  • Resend delivers each form submission to our team inbox by email. If you subscribe to our newsletter, your email address may be kept on a mailing list (an “audience”) in Resend. Read Resend’s privacy policy.
  • [Email hosting provider] hosts the Brandita team inbox where form submissions arrive.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or if Brandita’s business is reorganised or sold — in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

05 Cookies and analytics

The site’s own code sets no cookies, and it doesn’t store anything in your browser’s local storage.

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools: no tracking pixels and no third-party tags. Our fonts are served from our own site, so loading a page doesn’t send your details to Google Fonts or any other font service.

Depending on our security settings, Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary cookie when it needs to check that a visitor isn’t a bot. It’s used for security only, not for tracking or advertising.

Links on the site to WhatsApp, Google Maps and our social media profiles take you to services run by other companies. Nothing is shared with them until you click, and once you’re on their sites, their own privacy policies apply.

If we add analytics or any other cookies in future, we’ll update this policy first, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

07 How we protect it

  • The site is served over HTTPS, so what you type into our forms is encrypted on its way to us.
  • Form submissions aren’t stored in a database on the website. They’re passed straight on to our team inbox.
  • The key that lets our site send email is kept as an encrypted secret with our hosting provider, not in the website’s code.
  • Our forms filter out automated spam with an invisible field and a timing check. Neither identifies you, and neither is kept.

No system is completely secure. If something happens that puts your information at risk, we’ll tell you, and the relevant authorities, where the law requires us to.

08 International transfers

We work from Egypt and Oman, but our service providers operate internationally. Cloudflare runs a global network, so your request may be handled in a data centre outside your country, and its logs may be stored elsewhere. Resend processes the email it delivers for us in [Resend data region].

This means your information may be processed outside Egypt and Oman, including in countries whose data protection laws differ from yours. Both providers offer data processing terms that set out how they protect their customers’ data, including when it moves between countries.

09 Your rights

Depending on where you live, the law may give you some or all of these rights. You can ask us to:

  • Access — tell you what information we hold about you, and give you a copy.
  • Correct — fix anything that’s wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete — erase your information, unless we have to keep it for a legal reason.
  • Object — stop using your information for a particular purpose, including where we rely on our legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This doesn’t affect anything we did before you withdrew it.
  • Unsubscribe — leave the newsletter at any time, using the unsubscribe link in any newsletter email or by emailing us.

To make a request, email [privacy contact email]. We may need to confirm who you are before we act on it, and we’ll reply within the time the law requires.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

10 Children

This site is for businesses and the people who run them. It isn’t aimed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us their details, contact us and we’ll delete them.

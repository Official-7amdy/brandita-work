01

The partner..

Investment Guide entered the Omani market with an ambitious goal: to become the definitive reference for Arab investors looking for opportunity.

The reality it started from was the opposite. Zero digital presence. No brand identity. No marketing methodology. A completely fresh start, in a market where credibility is normally built over years of delivered work.

02

The challenge: building authority from nothing..

Most agencies would have advised a modest start — "testing the water". We took the challenge instead, because we had what it needed.

Where they started

  • Complete market invisibility: no identity, no website, no digital presence.
  • A credibility gap: zero case studies or proof of expertise, in an industry that runs entirely on trust.
  • Constrained resources: a limited budget for producing high-standard content in Oman.
  • No on-camera talent: no qualified Arabic-speaking presenter we could trust to build real relationships with investors.
  • A pricing problem: service costs above competitors', with no credibility yet to justify the premium position.
  • A sales gap: no sales team capable of handling the leads we were about to generate.

In investment services, credibility is revenue. Without a strategy that produced immediate market authority, Investment Guide would have stayed just another service provider competing on price.

03

What we did..

Phase 01

Building the foundation

When you're going after dominance in a new market from nothing, strategy is the whole game.

  • We studied the Omani market, the competitors and Arab investors before doing anything — looking for an approach that used our strengths, avoided our weaknesses, and handled the risks properly.
  • We started at the very first point of contact: the name. It wasn't chosen at random. It was selected to be the first search result for investing in Oman and for investor guidance in Oman — which put high SEO authority within reach from day one.
  • We designed an identity built on cultural-commercial fusion, combining the identity of the Arab investor with the culture of Omani business, to create an authentic connection with both the target market and the local one.
Phase 02

The 25-day sprint

The focus here was one thing: an integrated content system that would become the complete reference for investors making a serious decision.

On the website

  • Built the Investment Guide site as a full resource hub, answering every investment question in Oman — making it the first place a decision starts.
  • Produced a comprehensive investor guide — a full book — to establish credible expertise in the market.
  • SEO built to rank first across every investment-related query.

On social

  • Real value first: an 80% investor value, 20% selling, 0% empty marketing content split.
  • After an extensive search we found the right presenter and trained her over 15 intensive days into a credible, persuasive face for the brand with Arab investors.

Phase 04

The complete picture

We take on a partner's responsibility as if their business were ours, so the work didn't stop at generating leads.

  • A 30-day intensive training programme took the sales team from leaking qualified leads to converting them.
  • We worked alongside Investment Guide on service delivery, documenting client success and building strategic partnerships that extended reach in the market.

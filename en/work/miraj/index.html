04 · The machine

Three moves that fed the winning angle.

  1. The creators45creator videos delivered, Nov 2025 – Jul 2026

    A creator engine, every month.

    Creators briefed by collection (Classic, Premium, Elite, Memory foam, Kids, Quran, Ramadan), each video cut with four or five opening hooks to test.

  2. The codes245orders from five codes in March 2026: EGP 194,510; the best returned 43× its fee

    Influencers who sell, measured by their own codes.

    Each influencer posted on set days with a personal discount code, so every order was counted back to its source and the budget followed the ones who sold.

  3. The offer3 for 2“صلي براحة كأنك على سحابة” · Pray in comfort, as if on a cloud

    A reason to buy today.

    The golden offer, three rugs for the price of two, sold as a gift that stays with someone in every prayer; later, collection offers by range.

The next round, already written

Video openings

  • Open on the rug thrown and caught
  • Look into the camera, sitting on the floor
  • Start on a photo of the parents: the gift hook
  • Pull the rug from the back seat of the car
  • Calm music as the hook itself

Still images

  • Real customer reviews on the rugs
  • The item in the cart, the discount, “choose your third gift”
  • Two rugs side by side, right and wrong

06
The work

9 levers. Every one tested.

4 won, 1 did not. Tap a card for what happened.

  • 01Test → scaleCreative funnelTap to read

    Test, define, scale, duplicate. Every winner became the next round of tests.

  • 02Worked15% off wonMulti-offer testingTap to read

    Offers tested until one reached the order value the target ROAS needed for real profit.

  • 03Top 10% priceCore priceTap to read

    Priced above 90% of competitors, to reach the customer the brand was built for.

  • 04WorkedCar firstProduct selectionTap to read

    Car and home diffusers were both tested. Car brought more traffic and sales, so it got the focus.

  • 05WorkedHero: refillCross-sellTap to read

    The refill bottle at add-to-cart and in recommendations. It sold heavily.

  • 06EGP 969Order valueTap to read

    Target EGP 1,000 per order. The year averaged EGP 969.

  • 07Did not workDroppedFree shippingTap to read

    Free shipping over EGP 1,500 to lift basket size. It did not work.

  • 083 bundlesBundlesTap to read

    Winning scents, scents with the refill, and car plus home in one order.

  • 09Worked5.2× spendScalingTap to read

    Once tests were validated, spend went up and kept a profitable ROAS.

07
Where it started

A strong product, held back by its ads.

  • Limited acquisition at scale
  • High acquisition cost
  • Creative fatigue
  • Low conversion from cold traffic
  • Few winning angles
  • No way to scale without losing ROAS
What the audit found
  1. 01No follow-up from the previous media buyers
  2. 02Ad fatigue pushed cost per purchase up, and nothing was done about it
  3. 03No media plan, no creative plan, no offer tests, no pricing or competitor research
  4. 04A weak website experience
  5. 05Ad copy without real copywriting
  6. 06No campaign structure
08
What did not work

We publish the misses.

They are how the next test gets chosen.

  1. 01Did not workFree shipping over EGP 1,500 did not lift order value.

    It was meant to push baskets up. It did not, so it was dropped.

  2. 02Fell shortTarget EGP 1,000 per order. Landed at EGP 969.

    The refill cross-sell and bundles moved it close, not all the way.

  3. 03A dipOrders fell 11% in July.

    Spend dipped 7% and cost per purchase rose. August came back with 49% more orders.

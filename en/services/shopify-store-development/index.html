The process

How we do it..

  1. 01

    Scope and audit.

    What you're selling, to whom, in which markets, and what's blocking the sale today.

  2. 02

    Architecture.

    Catalogue structure, navigation, and the path to checkout.

  3. 03

    Design.

    Interface built on your brand system, designed around the buying decision.

  4. 04

    Build.

    Theme development, apps, integrations, tracking.

  5. 05

    Migrate and test.

    Data, URLs and redirects handled so you don't lose rankings on launch day.

  6. 06

    Launch and optimise.

    Post-launch monitoring and a CRO cycle, not a handover and goodbye.

FAQ

Questions we get asked..

How long does a Shopify build take?

Four to ten weeks, depending on catalogue size and migration complexity.

Will we lose SEO when migrating?

Not if it’s done properly — URL mapping and redirects are part of the scope.

Do we own the store?

Yes, entirely. It lives in your Shopify account from day one.

Do you support local payment gateways?

Yes, across Egyptian and Gulf gateways.

Do you handle Arabic stores?

Yes, including RTL layout, Arabic typography and bilingual catalogues.
